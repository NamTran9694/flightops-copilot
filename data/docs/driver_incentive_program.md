# Driver Retention and Safety Incentive Program (Synthetic)

Effective date: March 1, 2025.

## Background
In 2024 driver monthly attrition was between 5 and 8 percent, much higher than other roles. Each driver replacement costs about 3,500 dollars in recruiting, ramp licensing, and 2 weeks of paid training. Analysis showed driver pay was about 2 dollars per hour below nearby airport employers.

## Changes
1. Base hourly rate for all drivers increased by 2 dollars per hour.
2. Weekly safety incentive: a driver earns a 40 dollar bonus for each week with no incident, no audit failure, and on-time loading performance of 97 percent or higher.
3. Quarterly bonus of 150 dollars for drivers with 13 incident-free weeks in the quarter.

## How Incentives Are Calculated
Every Monday the analytics team combines the safety incident log, the weekly audit results, and on-time loading data per driver. The list of eligible drivers is sent to payroll before Wednesday. Disputes must be raised with the supervisor within 7 days.

## Expected Results
Reduce driver monthly attrition below 2 percent, reduce recordable incidents, and lower training cost.
