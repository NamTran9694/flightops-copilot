# Attrition Analysis Playbook (Synthetic)

## When to Run
Run every month, and immediately when any role is above its attrition target for two months in a row.

## Steps
1. Calculate monthly attrition by role: terminations in the month divided by active headcount at the start of the month.
2. Split terminations by tenure: under 90 days, 90 days to 1 year, over 1 year. Early leavers (under 90 days) usually point to hiring or onboarding problems. Leavers with longer tenure usually point to pay, schedule, or management problems.
3. Compare hourly pay with nearby employers for the same role.
4. Estimate the cost of attrition: replacements multiplied by the replacement cost (drivers about 3,500 dollars, other roles about 1,200 dollars).
5. Recommend actions and define a KPI to track the result.

## Example
In early 2025 driver attrition was the highest of all roles. The recommended actions were a 2 dollar per hour raise and a weekly safety incentive. After March 2025, driver attrition dropped to the 1 to 2 percent range.
