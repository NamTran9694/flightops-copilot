# Driver Safety Policy (Synthetic)

Applies to: all catering truck and highloader drivers working on the airport ramp.

## Speed and Movement on the Ramp
Maximum speed on the ramp is 15 mph and 5 mph within 25 feet of any aircraft. Drivers must stop completely before the aircraft safety envelope and use a guide person (wing walker) when reversing toward an aircraft.

## Highloader Docking
The highloader platform must be raised only after the truck is fully stopped, the parking brake is set, and the stabilizers are deployed. The bumper must stop at least 2 inches from the aircraft door frame. Any contact with the aircraft must be reported immediately to the supervisor and to the airline ramp control, even if no damage is visible.

## Weekly Safety Audits
Starting January 2025, supervisors audit about one third of active drivers every week. The audit checks speed, docking procedure, use of a guide person, cab cleanliness, and pre-trip vehicle inspection. Score 0 to 100; 80 or higher is a pass. A driver who fails two audits in a row must complete a 2-hour refresher training before the next shift.

## Incident Reporting
All incidents, including near misses, must be reported in the safety system before the end of the shift. Severity levels: near miss (no injury, no damage), first aid, recordable (requires medical treatment beyond first aid), and lost time.

## Fatigue
Drivers may not work more than 12 hours in a shift or more than 60 hours in a week, including overtime.
