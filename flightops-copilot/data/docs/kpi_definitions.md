# KPI Definitions – ATL Catering Facility (Synthetic)

Owner: Operations Analytics. Review cycle: quarterly.

## Labor Hours per Flight (H/F)
The primary labor productivity KPI. Formula: total paid labor hours (regular + overtime, all departments) divided by the number of flights catered on the same day. A lower value means labor is used more efficiently. Absolute headcount or total labor cost should not be compared month over month on their own, because flight volume changes; H/F normalizes labor by volume.

Targets: Green below 16.0 hours per flight, Yellow 16.0 to 17.0, Red above 17.0. Saturday values are expected to be about 4 percent higher because of lower flight volume and fixed staffing.

## Overtime Ratio
Overtime hours divided by total hours. Target: below 8 percent. Above 12 percent for two consecutive weeks triggers a staffing review with the department manager.

## Monthly Attrition Rate
Number of employees terminated during the month divided by the number of active employees at the start of the month. Reported per role. Target for drivers: below 2 percent per month. Target for other roles: below 3 percent per month.

## Incident Rate
Count of recordable safety incidents per month. Near misses and first-aid cases are tracked separately as leading indicators. Target: fewer than 1 recordable incident every 2 months.

## Safety Audit Pass Rate
Share of weekly driver safety audits with a score of 80 or higher. Target: above 85 percent.

## Catering Delay Rate
Share of flights with a catering-caused delay greater than zero minutes. Target: below 3 percent. Any delay over 15 minutes requires a root-cause note within 24 hours.
